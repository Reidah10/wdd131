The Jedi

Guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy.

The Jedi Order

The Jedi are the ancient order of peacekeepers of the galaxy, maintaining balance in the force and between peoples. The Jedi are trained from a young age to be attuned to the force and to use it for good.

Their relationship with the Republic became more and more complicated as the Clone Wars began because they were pushed to become soldiers and not the peacekeepers they were meant to be.

The Jedi

Qui-Gon Jinn

Qui-Gon Jinn was a wise and powerful Jedi Master who was known for his unorthodox methods and his strong connection to the Force. He was the master of Obi-Wan Kenobi and discovered Anakin Skywalker, believing him to be the Chosen One who would bring balance to the Force.

Obi-Wan Kenobi

Obi-Wan Kenobi was a wise and experienced Jedi Master who served as a mentor to both Anakin and Luke Skywalker. He was known for his skill with a lightsaber, his deep connection to the Force, and his contribution in the Clone Wars.

Anakin Skywalker

Anakin Skywalker was a legendary Jedi Knight who was prophesied to bring balance to the Force. He was the padawan and friend of Obi-Wan Kenobi, with whom he fought alongside in the Clone Wards. Later, he falls to the dark side, becoming Darth Vader.

Yoda

Yoda was the Grand Master of the Jedi Order and widely considered one of, if not the most powerful Jedi ever. He opposed the training of Anakin Skywalker as a boy but allowed for Obi-Wan to train him after Qui-Gon's death. During his exile to Dagobah he trained Luke Skywalker years after the fall of the Republic.

Luke Skywalker

Son of Anakin Skywalker and Padme Amidala, twin to Princess Leia Organa, leader of the rebellion. From moisture farmer to powerful Jedi, he trained with Obi-Wan Kenobi before his death on the Death Star at the hands of Darth Vader, later finding guidance from Yoda on Dagobah becoming a full fledged Jedi Knight.

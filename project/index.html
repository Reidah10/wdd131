Star Wars: Reign of the Empire

Experience the Fall of the Jedi and the Rise of the Empire as told through Episodes I-VI of Star Wars.

A Galaxy in Jeopardy

A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away...

Watch the galaxy quickly fall from peace into war, the discovery of the Chosen One, the rise of the Sith and their Empire, and the rebellion led by Luke Skywalker.

Episodes I-VI

The story starts with us following Jedi Knight Qui-Gon Jinn and his padawan Obi-Wan Kenobi on a mission to negotiate with the Trade Federation. When peace talks fail our heroes navigate their way through the galaxy from Naboo, meeting Jar Jar Binks and Padme Amidala, to Tatooine, where they find a boy named Anakin Skywalker. When fleeing the planet a mysterious man, later identified as Darth Maul, attacks the group. After successfully getting off Tatooine they find themselves back on Naboo and in the throws of war where Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan duel with Darth Maul where Qui-Gon ultimately loses his life and leaves Anakin in the care of Obi-Wan.

Ten years after the events of Episode I, we are reunited with our heroes Anakin and Obi-Wan who were assigned guard duty for the new Senator of Naboo, Padme Amidala. While Obi-Wan investigates a bounty hunter, Anakin tries to hide his love for Padme while serving as her bodyguard. While following the trail of Jango Fett, Obi-Wan discovers a clone army on the planet Kamino, supposedly commissioned by the Jedi. He follows Fett to the planet Geonosis where he is discovered by Count Dooku and taken captive. In an attemp to rescue his master, Anakin and Padme go to Geonosis where a full scale battle ensues between the Jedi and the Separatists. In order to win the battle, the Jedi call upon the Clone Army while Obi-Wan and Anakin pursue Dooku. Overwhelmed by Dooku, Obi-Wan and Anakin are saved by Grand Master Yoda, though Dooku manages to escape. Anakin and Padme are secretly married and thus we see the beginning of the Clone Wars.

A few years into the Clone Wars, we are reunited with our heroes. After rescuing the Chancellor, Obi-Wan investigates the Separatist army on Utapau and Anakin is tasked with spying on Palpatine for the Jedi Council. Plagued by visions of his wife's death, Anakin turns to Palpatine, who reveals himself as Darth Sidious and draws Anakin to the dark side. With Order 66, the Jedi are nearly exterminated, and Anakin attacks the Jedi Temple before heading to Mustafar to eliminate the Separatist leaders. Obi-Wan discovers Anakin's betrayal and follows Padme to Mustafar, where he confronts his former apprentice in a fateful duel. Obi-Wan emerges victorious, leaving Anakin crippled and dying. With Padme dead and the Republic fallen, Obi-Wan and Yoda go into hiding, separating Anakin's twins to protect them from the Empire.

Set 19 years after the events of Episode III, we are introduced to Luke Skywalker, a young farm boy on the planet Tatooine. After discovering a message from Princess Leia Organa hidden in a droid named R2-D2, Luke and his new friend Obi-Wan Kenobi set out to rescue the princess from the clutches of Darth Vader and the Empire. With the help of Han Solo and Chewbacca, they successfully rescue Leia and deliver the plans for the Death Star to the Rebel Alliance. In a final battle, Luke uses the Force to destroy the Death Star, bringing hope to the galaxy.

Following the destruction of the Death Star, the Empire retaliates by attacking the Rebel base on Hoth. Luke and his friends are forced to flee, with Luke seeking out Jedi Master Yoda for training. Meanwhile, Darth Vader relentlessly pursues Luke and his friends, leading to a climactic confrontation where Vader reveals a shocking truth about Luke's parentage.

In the final chapter of the original trilogy, Luke Skywalker confronts Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine in a bid to save his friends and bring balance to the Force. With the help of the Rebel Alliance, they launch a daring assault on the second Death Star. In a dramatic showdown, Luke redeems his father, Anakin Skywalker, who sacrifices himself to destroy the Emperor, leading to the ultimate defeat of the Empire and the restoration of peace to the galaxy.

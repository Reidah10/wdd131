Star Wars: Reign of the Empire

Site Name

Star Wars: Reign of the Empire

I chose this name for my project because I wanted to cover the story of episodes 1-6, the fall of the jedi and the rise of the empire, while showing some of my favorite characters both sith and jedi.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to inform the visitor abour the Star Wars universe and its defining story. It will briefly explain the events of Episodes 1-6 and dive into who the jedi and sith are, their beliefs, and important characters belonging to each faction.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

What happens in episode (user's choice) of Star Wars?

Scenario 2

What's the difference between the Sith and the Jedi?

Color Schema

I plan on using black and dark grey backgrounds with white for headings. For the main text color I'm going to use the classic Star Wars light blue and gold for accents, giving it that spacey feel with high contrast for easy visibility.

Typography

I plan on using Michroma for titles and headers and Saira Condensed for the body text.

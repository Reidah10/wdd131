The Sith

Dark Side practitioners of the Force, manipulating it for their own selfish desires.

The Sith Order

The Sith are the ancient order of dark side users, dedicated to using the Force for their own gain and power. They believe in the power of the dark side and seek to dominate the galaxy through fear and intimidation.

The Sith operate under the Rule of Two, meaning there are never more than two Sith at a time, a master, and their apprentice. They hide in the shadows, manipulating people with their cunning and perversion of the Force, seeking only power and influence for themselves.

The Sith

Darth Sidious

Darth Sidious was the secretive and powerful leader of the Sith, who orchestrated the rise of the Galactic Empire and the fall of the Republic. He was known for his cunning mind and his ability to manipulate others for his own gain.

Darth Vader

Darth Vader was the apprentice of Darth Sidious and one of the most feared warriors in the galaxy. Once a Jedi Knight named Anakin Skywalker, he fell to the dark side and became a symbol of the Sith's power and influence.

Count Dooku

Count Dooku was a former Jedi Master who became the leader of the Separatist movement and a Sith Lord under the tutelage of Darth Sidious. He was known for his skill with a lightsaber and his unconventional approach to the dark side. Dooku turned to the Sith after losing faith in the Jedi Order and the Republic.

Darth Maul

Darth Maul was the Zabrak Sith Apprentice to Darth Sidious and the first Sith seen in 1000 years. He was known for his iconic double-bladed lightsaber and his ruthless nature. Maul was a key figure in the early days of the Sith and played a significant role in the events leading up to the Clone Wars. He is the one who killed Qui-Gon Jinn on Naboo during the Duel of the Fates.
